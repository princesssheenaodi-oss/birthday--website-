One Last Thing

Hello there, Allie.

Gusto lang kitang batiin ng happy birthday. Alam mo, I'm genuinely really happy that I met you, kahit online lang and through a screen. I'm really glad that I have YOU. You're honestly one of my favorite people I've met online, and I'm really grateful that you became such a great friend to me.

Yung mga pag-uusap natin, especially yung mga random and funny things we talk about, always manage to make me smile. Masaya talaga ako tuwing kausap kita. I feel like I can be myself whenever I'm talking to you, and I really appreciate that because I don't feel like I have to pretend or act differently around you.

Birthday mo na ngayon, so I really hope you have a happy day. Ngumiti ka, kumain ka nang mabuti, enjoy yourself, and do the things that make you happy. And if you ever feel sad or things get difficult, please remember that I'm always here. You can always talk to me whenever you need someone.

Do you know I still remember the day you first talked to me on Instagram? I still remember it, even though I completely forgot what our conversation was about. I just remember that you said goodbye pretty quickly because you were going to sleep. And I still remember that you saw how down I was back then. Honestly, I thought we probably wouldn't talk again after that. But then I opened your story and replied to it, and somehow, doon na nagsimula yung madalas nating pag-uusap. I'm really glad I replied because I ended up meeting someone I genuinely enjoy talking to.

And I was really happy that you never ghosted me. Please keep being yourself, Allie. Keep being silly, keep being you, and keep sending me those random reels. I actually watch all the reels you send me because I want to know what's in them. Maybe somewhere in those random reels, I can also see a little bit of you. The things that make you laugh, the things you like, the things you're interested in, and the little things that make you happy. That's why I watch them all.

I hope we stay friends for a really long time. Sana walang iwanan. I hope we can keep talking, laughing, sending each other random things, and just being ourselves around each other for a long time. And even if you ever get mad at me someday, I hope we can still work things out and remain friends.

I hope you can do that because, honestly, I can't imagine just losing you and never talking to you again. This probably sounds dramatic, but yeah, I really don't want to lose you, Allie. You've become someone I genuinely care about, and I'm really grateful that I got to meet you.

That's it. Happy birthday, Allie. I hope you have a really good day, and I hope you continue to find more things that make you happy. Keep being yourself, keep being silly, and please don't ever feel like you have to change who you are.
